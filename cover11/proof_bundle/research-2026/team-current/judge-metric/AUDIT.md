Verdict: **chain analytically sound on the stated positive q0k4 domain, conditional on the assumed upstream
dependencies (9+2 C451, inward-root dominance C464, nearest-arc widths ≤ 2 asin R, positive widths,
q0k4 classification at R < .38).** I found no counterexample and no broken implication in the five
new claims. Below are the independent derivations of each load-bearing step and the exact interface

Scope of this audit: the five Markdown proofs only. The `check_*.py` files are declared "numeric

## 1. C835 (METRIC-GLUING-SUFFICIENCY) — flat development + covering: CONFIRMED

Premise: positive triangles, exact shared lengths, angle sums 2π at all 11 interior sites, boundary
edges 2 sin(w_i/2), boundary interior angles π−(w_i+w_(i+1))/2, 0<w_i<π, Σw_i=2π.

**1a. Flat development is single-valued.** Interior cone angle at each site = 2π, so the holonomy of
a loop around any interior vertex is rotation by 2π (identity, including the translational part: the
identification of the two developed slit edges about the vertex is exactly rotation by the cone angle
about the vertex image). Fundamental group of the disk minus interior vertices is generated by such
loops, hence all holonomy is trivial; the developing map is single-valued and orientation-preserving.
This is the standard flat-surface argument and it is used correctly.

**1b. Boundary is the canonical cyclic polygon.** Edge length 2 sin(w_i/2) and left-turn at P_i is
τ_i := (w_i+w_(i+1))/2 (verified: chord direction of edge i is θ_{i−1}+w_i/2+π/2; the turn to edge
i+1 is exactly (w_i+w_{i+1})/2). A polygonal chain is determined by (lengths, turns) up to rigid
motion; the canonical chain Q_i=(cos Σ_{j≤i}w_j, sin Σ_{j≤i}w_j) has the same data and closes because
Σw_i=2π. So the developed boundary is congruent to it, simple and strictly convex.

**1c. Degree argument.** Off the edge image the map is a local orientation-preserving diffeomorphism;
each preimage contributes +1, and the total degree equals the boundary winding number (1 inside the
convex polygon K, 0 outside). Hence the development is a homeomorphism onto K and all sites lie in
int K. Correct; no branch point at interior vertices because cone angle is exactly 2π.

**1d. Covering, four parts — all verified.**
- Site face: Σλ_j|x−c_j|² = ρ² − |x−O|². I recomputed: Σλ_j|x−c_j|² = Σλ_j|c_j|² − |x|² and
  Σλ_j|c_j|² = ρ² − |O|² + 2O·x, giving ρ² − |x−O|² ≤ R². Weighted average ≤ R² with λ≥0, Σλ=1
  forces one |x−c_j| ≤ R. Valid for obtuse faces too.
- Switch face: P and midpoint M=(ci+cj)/2 both inside both disks (|ci−cj| ≤ |ci−P|+|P−cj| ≤ 2R);
  convexity covers the two subtriangles. Correct.
- Sector face: three vertices in B(ci,R); convexity. Correct.
- Exterior segment: |P−c|²≤R² ⇒ c·P ≥ (1+|c|²−R²)/2 > 0 (R<1). For Z on the minor arc,
  Z = aP + bQ with a,b≥0, a+b≥1 (sine interpolation; recomputed a+b = cos(θ−mid)/cos(w/2)² ≥ 1).
  Then c·Z ≥ min(c·P,c·Q) and |Z−c|² ≤ R²; convex hull of arc = circular segment. Correct.

No missing planarity/circle-realization constraint: the flat development *is* the planar realization,
so the full equality system is genuinely sufficient (this is the key content of C835, and it holds).

## 2. C836 (ODD-BOUNDARY-SCALE-ELIMINATION) — CONFIRMED

**2a. Euler angle sum.** Ring interior equation is S_i + α_i − b_{i−1} − b_i = π (from
S_i + α_i + (π/2−b_{i−1}) + (π/2−b_i) = 2π). Summing over i with ΣS_i = 11π−4π = 7π gives
Σα_i = 2π + 2Σb_i; sector relation Σ(L_i+T_i) = 9π − Σα_i gives Στ_i = Σα_i − 2Σb_i = 2π.
This is automatic (discrete Gauss–Bonnet), not an extra equation. Correct.

**2b. Odd inversion.** (I+S)w = 2τ is uniquely invertible for n=9; inverse = (I−S+S²−…−S⁸)/2, so
w_i = Σ_{k=0}^8 (−1)^k τ_{i+k}. Direct check: w_i+w_{i+1} telescopes to 2τ_i (the k=1..8 terms cancel
and the two τ_i boundary terms add). Correct. Σw_i = Στ_i = 2π automatically.

**2c. Width-domain check is genuinely separate.** Positive τ_i does **not** imply w_i ∈ (0,π); the
alternating sum can be non-positive. The claim keeps this as an explicit domain condition. Correct
(and the registered control counterexample is consistent).

**2d. Circle closure ⇔ 8 ratio equalities.** With w_i defined by τ_i=(w_i+w_(i+1))/2 and Σw=2π, the
developed boundary is cyclic of radius R iff ℓ_i = 2R sin(w_i/2), i.e. R_i := 2 sin(w_i/2)/ℓ_i all
equal. Correct. The recovered R = R_i is the physical allowance after the intrinsic lengths are
normalized by R (intrinsic ℓ_i = physical/R = 2 sin(w_i/2)/R).

**2e. Uniform scaling ⇒ max local radius = 1 at a minimizer.** Scaling all intrinsic lengths by s
preserves angles/ratios and sends R→R/s, so a minimizer cannot have max < 1. Claim correctly says
only max=1, never all-saturated. Correct.

## 3. C837 (POSITIVE-METRIC-INFIMUM = global infimum) — CONFIRMED

**3a. Strict-allowance construction.** Cover at r<.38 ⇒ (C451) two hubs strictly interior; pick
R∈(r, min(.38, 1−|hub|)) so hubs remain strictly interior at R. Same centers at radius R strictly
cover the boundary (nearest distance ≤ r < R). C464 inner-root replacement preserves each covered
arc as a *strict* set: recomputed |P−dn|²−R² = 2d(cos a − P·n) with d² − 2d cos a + (1−R²) = 0,
so sign(|P−dn|²−R²) = sign(cos a − P·n) is independent of the root d>0. Hence strict boundary
coverage survives, and the inner root has norm ≤ √(1−R²). Correct.

**3b. Positivity of all 29 triangles (for R>r).**
- Site faces: three distinct cocircular Delaunay sites; a line meets a circle in ≤2 points, so
  noncollinear. Circumradius ρ = Voronoi radius = nearest-center distance at an interior Voronoi
  vertex ≤ R by disk covering. Valid given the q0k4 refined triangulation has interior Voronoi
  vertices (assumed upstream).
- Switch faces: equidistant Pi and midpoint M of interior ci,cj; |M|<1 (strict convexity) while
  |Pi|=1, so Pi ≠ M, hence Pi ∉ line(ci,cj). Nondegenerate. Correct.
- Sector faces: height k−n·ci with k=cos(w_i/2) ≥ √(1−R²) (from w_i ≤ 2 asin R) ≥ |ci| (inner root).
  Equality forces w_i=2 asin R, ci=√(1−R²)n, hence both endpoints at distance exactly R —
  contradicting strict coverage. So height > 0. Correct. (Strict coverage at the *switch points*
  holds because the switch points are boundary points and the strict arc sets are identical under
  C464; verified in §3a.)

**3c. Infimum equality.** S-assignments with recovered radius R<.38 produce genuine radius-R covers
(C835), so r_D ≤ inf_S R. Conversely every genuine cover at r<.38 produces S at R↓r, so
inf_S R ≤ r, and inf over covers gives inf_S R ≤ r_D. Equality of infima; no attainment needed.
The singular formal branches are therefore irrelevant for a lower bound. Correct.

**Interface note (load-bearing):** this transfer uses the *full* q0k4 model (shared lengths + angle
sums + circle ratios), not an independent-factor relaxation. If later optimization relaxes any of
these, the equality r_D = inf_S R no longer holds.

## 4. C838 (SIX-INDEPENDENT-CIRCLE-EQUATIONS) — CONFIRMED

Given the flat development closes the boundary, Σℓ_i v_i = 0; the canonical polygon with the same
turns τ_i=(w_i+w_(i+1))/2 and edge lengths q_i=2 sin(w_i/2) closes, Σq_i v_i = 0, with the same
direction vectors v_i (turns determine directions). Define R=q_0/ℓ_0, impose Rℓ_i=q_i for i=1..6.
Subtracting R·(closure) from the canonical closure leaves
(Rℓ_7−q_7)v_7 + (Rℓ_8−q_8)v_8 = 0.
v_7,v_8 differ by turn τ_7=(w_7+w_8)/2 ∈ (0,π) (strict because w_i∈(0,π)), hence independent;
both coefficients vanish. Correct. The two omitted equalities follow exactly, not by numerical rank.

Load-bearing condition: Σℓ_i v_i = 0 requires the angle sums to be satisfied **exactly**
(flat development exists); "residual-small" angle sums do not license the cancellation. The claim
states this explicitly; it is the correct discipline.

## 5. C839 (ANGLE-ONLY-EXACT-MODEL) — CONFIRMED

**5a. Factor graph.** Nodes = 11 site faces + 9 switches = 20; edges = 12 internal site-site + 9
ring site-switch = 21; connected; cycle rank 21−20+1 = 2. Independent re-derivation from the face
list confirms: site-face adjacency has the 6-cycle F0–F1–F2–F3–F10–F9–F0 (around hub 9) and the
7-cycle F9–F8–F7–F6–F5–F4–F10–F9 (around hub 10), with 9 switch leaves attached to F_i along ring
edges (i,i+1). Exactly 2 independent cycles.

**5b. Sine-law matching.** For a shared site-site edge: 2γ_f sin a_fg = 2γ_g sin a_gf. For a ring
edge shared with a switch, the isosceles switch gives side = 2 y_i sin(b_i), so the switch is encoded
by (γ_sw = y_i, a_sw = b_i). The 21 equations are linear in the 20 γ's; connected graph ⇒ solution
space is 1-dimensional (y = t γ) provided the 2 cycle products equal 1. Correct.

**5c. Objective.** Sector chord ℓ_i scales linearly in t; six circle ratios are scale-free (q_i
independent of t, ℓ_i ∝ t). Local radii ≤ 1 ⟺ t ≤ 1/M, M=max γ_f. R = q_0/(t ℓ_0) minimized at
t = 1/M ⇒ R_shape = M q_0/ℓ_0. Root gauge rescales γ, ℓ, M together, so R_shape and the constraints
are root-invariant. Correct.

**5d. Count.** 42 angles = 33 site + 9 switch-half; 13 linear (11 face sums + 2 hub sums), 2 sine
cycles, 6 circle ratios ⇒ 21-dimensional generically. Consistent with C838. Correct.

## 8. Conclusion

No concrete proof gap found in C835–C839 under the stated assumptions. The four hidden implications
that are genuinely load-bearing and must be preserved downstream are:
(1) angle sums/shared lengths must be **exact** for C835's development and C838's cancellation;
(2) w_i ∈ (0,π) is a separate domain check, not implied by positive turns;
(3) the infimum equality in C837 uses the **full** simultaneous system, not a relaxed dual;
(4) C837's positivity of sector triangles uses strict coverage (r < R), which is why the allowance
argument is needed and why singular strata do not have to be optimized separately.
