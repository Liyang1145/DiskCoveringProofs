# Literature interface (Round8): circle-pattern variational principles

1. Bobenko–Springborn, Variational principles for circle patterns and Koebe's theorem, TransAMS356(2004)659–689. DOI10.1090/S0002-9947-03-03239-2. Source https://doi.org/10.1090/s0002-9947-03-03239-2 . Search/read excerpt confirms strictly convex Euclidean functional in log face radii modulo scale, for FIXED circle intersection angles and cone angles. Coherent-angle linear feasibility characterizes existence. Not a theorem minimizing maximum covering radius with free intersection angles.
2. Springborn, A variational principle for weighted Delaunay triangulations and hyperideal polyhedra, arXiv:math/0603097, https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.math/0603097 . Coherent triangle angles with linear sums do not automatically glue: nonlinear scale compatibility appears as criticality of volume functional. This directly corroborates why C839's two sine-product cycles cannot be dropped. Weighted version assumes nonintersecting vertex disks, unlike our overlapping covering disks; no direct application.

Possible route: parameterize fixed edge circumcircle-intersection angles, use strict-convex inner solution to remove metric compatibility, then optimize outer intersection-angle parameters. Missing implication: radius objective convexity/monotonicity in free angles and unit-circle boundary matching are NOT provided by existence/uniqueness theorem. Do not conclude our global minimizer unique/symmetric from fixed-angle uniqueness. No novelty claim for gluing/angle compatibility machinery; our q0k4 application and odd9boundary scale elimination are explicit elementary reductions.
