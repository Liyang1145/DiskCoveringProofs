# Positive metric gluing suffices for disk covering (lead analytic proof draft)

## Exact object/domain

Faces11site triangles (9hub/ring plus2hub/hub/ring),9switch triangles(ci,c(i+1),Pi),9sector triangles(P(i−1),ci,Pi). Assign a positive Euclidean triangle to each face. Require exact shared edge lengths. Interior angles sum2pi at all11site vertices. Let positive widths wi∈(0,pi),sumwi=2pi. Boundary edge P(i−1)Pi has length2sin(wi/2); interior angle atPi is pi−(wi+w(i+1))/2. Degenerate triangles are NOT covered by the proof as stated. They need compact-limit treatment only when known to be limits of feasible positive metrics; do not assume every formal degenerate assignment is such a limit. Necessary relaxation with degeneracies remains valid regardless.

## A. Flat development
Glue triangles along shared edges abstractly, orientation preserving as surfaces. Interior points off vertices have Euclidean neighborhoods. At each interior vertex total2pi makes the cone Euclidean. Thus the open disk carries a flat oriented Euclidean structure, hence a path-independent developing map toR²: continue one rigid triangle placement across edges. Loops in dual graph are generated by circuits around interior vertices (the disk has no handles); crossing such a circuit rotates by2pi around the same vertex, hence Euclidean holonomy identity, including translation. Equivalently contract loops in the interior flat disk. The map extends continuously across edges/vertices and the boundary. Every face maps orientation positively.

The boundary is locally convex since prescribed angle<pi. More precisely, its edge lengths and successive left-turn angles are EXACTLY those of the canonical cyclic polygon with vertices Q_i=(cos(sum_{j<=i}wj),sin(sum_{j<=i}wj)). AtPi turn=(wi+w(i+1))/2. Align the first directed edge with its canonical chord. Induction across9edges puts every boundary vertex at correspondingQ_i (up to rigid motion); endpoint closure comes from sumwi2pi. Therefore boundary is simple strictly convex, traversed once CCW.

For y not on image of an edge, number of preimages under developed faces equals oriented degree, equal winding number of this simple boundary:1 inside convex polygonK,0 outside. Each preimage contributes+1 since face maps preserve orientation. So interiors of developed triangles partitionK with multiplicity1; none lie outsideK. For points on edges/vertices, flat interior local homeomorphism and half-disk boundary local injectivity exclude additional identifications, or follow by nearby regular-point degree. Thus development is a homeomorphism ontoK and all site vertices lie inintK.

## B. Each part is covered
Site triangle: let circumcenterO,radius rho<=R, x=Σlambda_j c_j in triangle. Identity Σlambda_j|x−c_j|²=rho²−|x−O|²<=R² implies at least one |x−c_j|<=R. This proof works for obtuse faces too.

Switch triangle(ci,cj,P): P within both endpoint disks. MidpointM=(ci+cj)/2 also within both by triangle inequality |ci−cj|<=|ci−P|+|P−cj|<=2R. Convexity puts triangle(ci,M,P) inB(ci,R) and triangle(cj,M,P) inB(cj,R).

Sector triangle(Pprev,ci,P): all3vertices are inB(ci,R); convexity covers it.

Exterior circular segment over boundary chordPQ: write w∈(0,pi), endpointsP,Q, ring centerc, |P−c|,|Q−c|<=R<1. Both dot products c·P,c·Q >= (1+|c|²−R²)/2>0. Every intermediate minor-arc point Z has representation Z=aP+bQ, a,b>=0,a+b>=1 (sine interpolation). Hence c·Z>=min(c·P,c·Q), so |Z−c|²<=max(|P−c|²,|Q−c|²)<=R². Disk convexity covers the circular segment (convex hull of arc). K plus these9segments is full unitD; interiors of segments disjoint. Thus11disks coverD.

## C. Factor interface
Switch face angles: atPi2bi, at its two sites pi/2−bi; sector face angles L_i,alpha_i,T_i, with chord widthwi. Interior ring total2pi is sum(siteface angles)+alpha_i−b(i−1)−bi=pi. Hub total2pi unchanged. Boundary prescribed angle is exactly T_i+2bi+L_(i+1)=pi−(wi+w_(i+1))/2 (C820). Side matching is exactly all21site edges plus18endpoint copies in established121column factor model. Triangle sine-law and q chord relations give the Euclidean triangles required above.

Consequently the FULL simultaneous equality/inequality system (not independent-factor dual relaxation) is sufficient on its positive domain, as well as necessary for genuine positive inward-root diagrams. No separate planarity/circle-realization constraint is missing there. The remaining failure of bounds comes from relaxation/optimization, not unknown nondegenerate gluing conditions. Boundary convexity/degree proof crucial: angle sums alone on arbitrary topology do not suffice.
