# Fixed-U branch exclusions: enlarge then reclassify

Analytic assembly interface, not a claim that branch certificates exist.

Let U denote the exact rational C934 upper threshold. Suppose one proves:
E10: no12-disk cover of D at exactly radiusU with exactly10 actual boundary-active disks.
E11: no12-disk cover of D at exactly radiusU with exactly11 actual boundary-active disks.
Then every cover at any r≤U has exactly9 actual boundary-active disks, both before and after enlargement toU.

Proof: Project centers intoD first; this preserves coverage, but rebuild all active labels. At r≤U C926 gives k∈{9,10,11}. Increase all disk radii toU keeping centers fixed. Coverage remains and the actual active count k' is nondecreasing. AtU C926 again gives k'≤11 (boundary-active norms≥1−U>U cannot cover0, so some inactive hub exists). E10/E11 force k'=9; thus9≤k≤k'=9. This proves the statement.

After enlargement and reclassification, the inactive hub norms satisfy |h|<1−U automatically; their whole radiusU disks lie strictly insideD. One may then perform same-arc inner-root replacement AT U on the active disks, preserving coverage and their new actual arcs. This validates fixed-U exposed-hub-union capacity arguments on their intended legal domain.

WARNING: Enlarging toU while keeping the OLD active/inactive labels does NOT guarantee old hubs remain strictly insideD as radiusU disks. Conversely no sharp optimum proof may replace the original radiusr in an equality-constrained contact chart without a proved inequality direction. E10/E11 are only coarse exclusion steps; once exact9 is established, rebuild the k9 dual at original radiusr for the sharp network argument.
