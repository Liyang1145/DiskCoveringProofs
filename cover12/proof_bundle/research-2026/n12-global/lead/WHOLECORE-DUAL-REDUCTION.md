# One-small-hub exposed-arc dual (proposed strengthening)

Suppose one hub h has e≤e0=3U−1, so it can cover the core; other hub g arbitrary inactive, distance d=2s∈(0,2U). Its overlap removes from S(h,U) an arc centered along g−h, with halfwidth b=acos(s/U). Normalize direction h along+x and let gamma be angle ofg−h. With onehub weights W=b0+bc cosφ+bs sinφ, V=v0+vc cosψ+vs sinψ, demand for S1 and the exposed part of S(h,U) is
req=π(b0+v0)−[v0 b+sin b(vc cosγ+vs sinγ)].
Capture of any ring is bounded above by its capture on the full two circles (the k11 formula). If 10supC<req this excludes the twohub configuration, without doing arc intersections at all. All densities remain nonnegative. This is a sufficient relaxed dual, not an equivalence.

In hub-line chart h=h1=(x−s,y), g=h2=(x+s,y), directiong−h=+x. After rotating h onto+x, cosγ=(x−s)/e, sinγ=−y/e. Existing onehub aggregate hasvs=0,vc≤0. Thus when x≥s occultation lies on the lower-density forward side; when x<s it lies on the higher-density backward side. At e=0 use uniform V (available first onehub weights set vc=0). The second hub norm bound gives only further constraints; no assumption is made that x≥s follows from whole-core coverage.
