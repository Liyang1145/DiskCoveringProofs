# Literature route: circumcircle compatibility, not claimed solution

Search 2026-09-27 for Euclidean circle pattern radius variational principles.
- Bobenko & Springborn, Variational principles for circle patterns and Koebe's theorem, PDF https://page.math.tu-berlin.de/~bobenko/papers/2003_Bob_Spr.pdf . Existence/uniqueness for given intersection angles/cone angles; convex functional in logarithmic radii. Boundary and cone singularities included.
- Springborn thesis, Variational principles for circle patterns, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312363 . Delaunay circumcircle patterns, Euclidean convex variational principle; spherical case differs.
- Springborn, A variational principle for weighted Delaunay triangulations and hyperideal polyhedra, https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.math/0603097 . Coherent angle systems linear; nonlinear metric compatibility recovered as stationarity of variational functional. Explicitly warns local/global Delaunay issues on general piecewise-flat surfaces.
