# Original-candidate curvature/dual energy interface

This is a proposed extension on the actual retained candidate angle boxes.
It is not a replacement for the original local lower-bound barrier, a generic
complete-type proof, or the unrestricted covering reduction.

First semantically reconstruct the accepted candidate angular tree and every
prior force, local, empty and Dirichlet payment. The incoming retained rows
must equal the actual current PENDING-ANGLES.json exactly, including original
leaf ownership, closed bounds and sharp bounds. The original root, rotations,
52-center-coordinate mapping and local allowance 7/800000 remain unchanged.

Use exactly the original accepted positive 92-rod support, with 16 anchors and
46 grounded free vertices, not the 134-rod generic complete-complex graph.
The current Dirichlet source gate matches this graph to the reconstructed
same-root covering geometry and independently checks every integer inverse
identity and Schur boundary energy coefficient. The new adapter explicitly
requires the original 62-vertex dimensions and original kernel schema.
Neither the original pinning reference nor its weights are changed.

For arbitrary realizations of those rods at radius at most Rstar, positive
weights give total rod energy at most Rstar_upper² times their weight sum.
Ground-node square completion bounds this total energy below by the Schur
energy of the anchors. For anchors at unit-circle turns theta, that energy is
the nonnegative weighted sum of 2[1-cos(2pi(theta_j-theta_i))].

The already independently checked graph-independent quadratic bound may be
applied only after this explicit original-candidate adapter gate. On each
actual closed sharp angular box, exact trigonometric intervals and the
necessary difference matrix give an outward Hessian minorant. Exact positive
LDL, including the complete rational-rounding spectral error, verifies a
coordinatewise nonnegative negative-curvature allowance.

At an exact feasible reference, the reference energy, interval gradient and
nonnegative difference-constraint dual give a whole-domain lower bound after
charging either the negative quadratic remainder or its valid box-secant
minorant. The exact reference must lie in the closed box and satisfy every
necessary difference constraint. Solver objectives, eigenvalues and success
markers only suggest certificates and never establish a payment.

Only a strict lower bound greater than the same-root rod energy upper bound
can exclude an incoming candidate box. Equality does not pay. Every retained,
unvisited or unproved incoming closed box stays unchanged. Original paid nodes,
complete closed splits and shared boundaries remain intact under a separate
overlay ledger. A feasible root-containing tiny candidate box must not be
falsely called empty, and its original accepted LOCAL proof remains binding.

Standalone exact acceptance, actual published replay, explicit source
ownership and a fresh complete current-content audit are required before any
new candidate box is removed. Discovery output alone removes no box and does
not prove the global covering lower bound.
