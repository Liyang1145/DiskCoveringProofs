all source OPEN boxes must match the published original full ledger. Reused

actual closed source box with the sealed standard-library correlated force

the same certified Rstar upper endpoint. Equality and all other nonstrict or

energy or hybrid payment. A full type is excluded only when all its source
OPEN boxes have been paid. The canonical terminal filter removes exactly the
actual current members of newly closed types; all previous exclusions,
unaffected states and both candidate isomorphic paths remain unchanged.

dimensions, changed boxes, duplicate/omitted ownership, partial graph and

unconditional U. Full catalogue completion, candidate angular closure, final
