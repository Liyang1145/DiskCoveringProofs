# Alternative positive supports on the original rod graph

This is a NEW support, not a relabelled original fixed-weight kernel.
It uses exactly the same 92 necessary rod edges and 62 vertices, with the
same 16 boundary anchors and 46 grounded free vertices.

For any explicitly positive rational weights w_e on those edges, a rod
realization of radius at most Rstar necessarily satisfies

    sum_e w_e ||z_u-z_v||^2 <= Rstar_upper^2 sum_e w_e.

Minimizing the left side over all free vertex coordinates only weakens this
necessary condition. Exact grounded elimination produces the boundary Schur
energy. The new module checks the integer inverse identity, harmonic convex
combination identities and Schur identities, and compares the rod edges
exactly against the original model reference. It does not use the old
original inverse with the new weights.

Outward sine/cosine intervals and the existing global second-derivative
remainder give valid closed-angle-box lower bounds. The upper energy uses
the NEW weight sum and scale, and the unchanged exact same-root radius.
Different supports may cover different closed regions. An exact partition
and preservation of every shared boundary would still be required to
lower-bound an entire root. An original LOCAL region is not an emptiness
claim and its 7/800000 coordinate allowance must remain unchanged.

The root28 diagnostic demonstrates that the old fixed-energy-limit point
is not a limitation of every positive support. A freshly optimized and
then EXACTLY reconstructed positive support excludes that point and one
proper point-containing neighborhood. The optimizer reached its iteration
limit, but its convergence is irrelevant: positivity, the exact inverse,
and the exact strict energy margin are the proof obligations for this
arithmetic diagnostic.

The fixed arithmetic resource is DATA only. No new whole-root partition,
complete latest source/model/history gate, frozen geometry acceptance,
publication replay, complete-current admission, or final independent rehash
has yet been provided for this NEW support mode. No root is formally paid,
and no rod realization or continuous-cover counterexample is constructed.
