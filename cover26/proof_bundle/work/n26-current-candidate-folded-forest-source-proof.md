# Exact continuation source after complete candidate forests

This interface creates no geometric payment. It identifies the actual
candidate roots on which a subsequent ENERGY/LOCAL forest may operate.

The input is the accepted standalone forest, its actual published replay,
the complete current audit and that audit's independent final digest
recheck. All bytes of the audit inventory and the standalone acceptance are
rehased. The standalone's original curvature input is reconstructed through
its own sealed input gate. The curvature gate is not given a forest bundle.

The accepted forest is replayed using the original 92 positive rods, original
grounded inverse, same root Rstar and original 52-coordinate local barrier.
Every closed split, shared midpoint, leaf certificate and retained whole
root is reconstructed. All published semantic outputs must match this replay.
The original tiny feasible root control is replayed and must remain feasible
for the energy screen; the original LOCAL entry allowance remains 7/800000.

The paid whole-root indices must be distinct. Their complementary original
rows, in original order and with unchanged full closed bounds, must equal the
current pending file. A partly paid root remains pending in its entirety.
A LOCAL leaf proves a radius lower bound and is never an emptiness payment.
Every proof-time parent byte must be retained identically in the actual
current audit, and the root radius and candidate count must agree.

For the first accepted forest this folds 37 incoming roots into 3 paid roots
(indices 1123, 1001 and 997) and 34 unchanged remaining roots. Future discovery
may select only the 34 remaining original indices. It cannot pay those three
roots again or silently return to the historical 37-root source.

The continuation producer searches finite closed-half forests with fresh
stationary references and outward common-denominator curvature allowances.
Floating calculations only propose certificates and split priorities.
ENERGY leaves still need the unchanged exact bound, exact positive LDL,
full reconstruction, charged rounding error, nonnegative difference dual
and strict margin. All OPEN and unvisited leaves remain explicit.
A proposal is not an acceptance or a new current audit.

Global combinatorial enumeration, unrestricted cover reduction and joint
acceptance remain separate pending obligations. This source gate establishes
neither the global lower bound nor optimality.
