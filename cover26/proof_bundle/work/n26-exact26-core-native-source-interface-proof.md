## 2. Root-face peeling exhausts original simple disk triangulations

Take one pending simple polygonal disk region with ordered boundary and J
undiscovered original interior vertices. Its distinguished first boundary

The triangle's third vertex is either:

- An existing vertex of that region boundary. The two remaining polygonal

- An undiscovered interior vertex of that region. Assign it the next label
