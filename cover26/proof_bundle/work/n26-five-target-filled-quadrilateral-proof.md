# Five core targets outside a filled consecutive-owner quadrilateral

This is a distinct mathematical rule, not part of the already frozen
six-target triangular-ear main. It creates no formal payment by itself.
The original-owner/strict-slack/embedded-dual or corner-starred-limit
premises and the imported original-nonowner family count remain explicit.

Let U=234701/1000000 and R=1-2U. A single original owner arc has length
at most2asin(rho)<pi/6 for rho<=U, since U<1/4<sin(pi/12).
The latter radical comparison follows from sqrt(3)<7/4.

Consider a fully filled center-disk subcomplex with simple boundary
i,i+1,i+2,i+3 in original cyclic owner order. Align x with the unit anchor
between i and i+1. The first two owners are within rho of that anchor.
The third is within rho of the next anchor, less than pi/6 away.
The fourth is within rho of the following anchor, less than pi/3 away.
Hence every one of the four center x-projections is at least

h=cos(pi/3)-U=1/2-U.

A filled simple polygon, convex or not, lies inside the convex hull of
its boundary vertices. Therefore every enclosed center has x>=h.
The same inclusion holds for corner-shadow embedded limits by continuity.
Since U<1/4, h>U.

Take FIVE exact core targets:

O=(0,0), T+=(0,R), T-=(0,-R),
L+=(-sqrt(3)R/2,R/2), L-=(-sqrt(3)R/2,-R/2).

All lie in the closed core disk; every pair has distance at least R>2U.
Their x-coordinates are nonpositive. No disk centered in the filled
quadrilateral can cover any target, because horizontal distance>=h>U.
The original boundary owners miss the core (or its open interior, followed
by finite-closed-union closure), so FIVE DISTINCT ORIGINAL nonowners
outside the patch must cover the targets.

If S is the set of enclosed nonowner labels, all outside original disks
have distinct retained labels among the26-B-|S| other slots. This budget
includes introduced outside labels and all future labels. Shadow placements
can only reduce the number of original disks available. Thus if
26-B-|S|<=4 the entire geometric prefix and all its continuations are
impossible.

Enclosed-label thresholds: B14:8, B15:7, B16:6, B17:5.
This differs from a triangular ear: the larger filled patch need not
contain a consecutive-owner triangle enclosing enough labels.

Finite recognition must check a COMPLETE filled triangular disk with
EXACT four-cycle boundary, no other original owner vertex, connected
face dual, correct edge incidence/Euler and path/circle vertex links.
An open four-sided region that future faces could fill is not paid.
Continuous coverage, projection and embedding are written arguments,
not conclusions from the finite scan.
