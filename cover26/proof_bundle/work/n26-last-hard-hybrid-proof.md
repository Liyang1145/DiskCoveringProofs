# Additive hybrid closure on an explicitly unchanged source OPEN

The actual current residual overlay and its matching published standalone
replay are bound. Its source gate and derive operation reconstruct all chosen
full force trees, prior energy payments, and accepted residual closed forests.
The complete current classification, pending boxes, leaf ledger, and terminal
frontier must match this reconstruction.

Discovery was made against the older quadratic overlay, not against this
current residual overlay. Its recorded older acceptance hash must equal the
current residual overlay's source acceptance hash. The entire target type
classification, pending record, complete OPEN dictionary, and full graph must
be unchanged. The exact difference matrix is from the same bound registry.
This explicit lineage does not silently relabel discovery.

New positive rational kernels are an additive proof layer. Every supplied
kernel, including unused discovery kernels, must pass the unchanged full-graph,
strict weight positivity, exact grounded inverse, harmonic row sum, and Schur
sign/symmetry gates. The older energy-only forest's kernel-equality gate is
not modified. Floating proposal objectives and spectral diagnostics are not
inputs to acceptance.

The new hybrid traversal supports exact energy and correlated-force leaves.
Both recompute strict whole-closed-box radius lower bounds through the sealed
standard-library engines. Correlated force recomputes exact integer divergence,
outward norm bounds, nonnegative difference duals, interval derivative errors,
and full arc curvature remainder. Energy recomputes Hessian minorants with
rounding charges, exact positive LDL and reconstruction, feasible reference,
nonnegative difference duals with every residual, curvature remainder, and
outward radius conversion with both squares checked.

Every proper interior rational split retains both closed halves. All nodes
must be reachable once. A separate breadth-first traversal with independently
implemented domain contraction must reproduce every depth-first leaf box.
EMPTY is permitted only for exact contradiction. OPEN is always retained.
A source parent is removed only when all its closed leaf domains are paid.
A canonical type is excluded only when no source or replacement OPEN remains.
The terminal filter must remove exactly the actual current members of newly
closed types while preserving all prior removals and both candidate paths.

Feasible radius-one energy and force controls, exact energy identity controls,
and mutations of kernels, graph, duals, split halves and parent ownership are
checked. All conclusions have scope radius at most the same certified Rstar,
not unconditional U. Full catalogue completion, candidate angular closure,
final unrestricted reduction and global optimality remain unproved.
