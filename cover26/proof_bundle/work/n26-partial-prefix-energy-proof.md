# Partial-prefix energy: conditional whole-subtree exclusion

This is a new proof interface, not an alteration of the sealed full-type
kernel. The intended source is an actual accepted radius-at-most-Rstar
transport frontier. Global arbitrary-cover reduction and final joint
acceptance remain separate obligations.

## Ownership and necessary present rods

Resolve each proposed (B, prefix) using the independent integer prefix
reference and match the entire actual source record: introduced-center count,
present faces, adjacency, pending polygons, immediate-child count and terminal
flag. A source identifier is not a substitute for equality of this record.
Complete candidate terminal paths and their isomorphic members are retained.

For a prefix with b anchors, m introduced centers and f present triangular
faces, use exactly b+m+f vertices. Each anchor is connected to its two incident
boundary centers; each present face gets one free witness connected to its
three already introduced centers. No unintroduced center, unpresent face,
future edge, complete-type padding or virtual angular payment is added.
The new independent construction must match the existing necessary rod
construction. The separate transport interface supplies the implication
that represented covers of radius rho realize every present rod with length
at most rho.

Peeling extensions keep introduced labels and all old faces. Therefore any
descendant realization restricts to a realization of these present rods.
A strict lower bound on all present-prefix realizations excludes the whole
prefix subtree, but never silently changes an original-U frontier or supplies
the missing unrestricted covering theorem.

## Exact positive grounded kernel

Let every present rod have a strictly positive rational weight w_e and let
L be its weighted Laplacian. Positivity is checked as integer numerators over
a positive integer scale. Every free vertex must be connected to an anchor.
Consequently the grounded free block A is positive definite: its quadratic
form is a sum of positive weighted edge squares with anchor coordinates zero,
and equality propagates zero along paths to the anchors.

The supplied inverse is checked by every entry of A A_inverse = identity,
using integer products, and by symmetry and positive diagonals. Harmonic
coefficients must be nonnegative and sum to one. Schur boundary rows must sum
to zero, be symmetric and have nonpositive off-diagonal entries.

For each coordinate, completing the square gives

E = x_anchor^T S x_anchor
    + (x_free − H x_anchor)^T A (x_free − H x_anchor).

The second term is nonnegative. For unit-circle anchors with turns theta,
the Schur term equals the nonnegative-pair expression

Phi(theta) = sum_(i<j) 2 c_ij [1 − cos(2 pi (theta_j − theta_i))].

Thus E >= Phi(theta), while rod lengths at most rho imply
E <= rho² sum_e w_e. The independent two-coordinate identity control checks
direct rod sums against the Schur-plus-residual formula at exact rational,
nonharmonic points. A radius-one rod realization is also checked.
These are regression controls; source ownership and universal inequalities
are still required for an actual transfer.

## Closed angular-domain lower bound

Use the same-root outward chord caps, checked by exact sine intervals.
Rebuild the necessary anchor difference matrix by two independent center-path
and difference-closure constructions. Each retained root box is closed,
contains the full necessary angular domain, and fixes theta_0=0.
An empty domain needs its own exact reconstruction and explicit transfer.

The existing bound arithmetic is graph-independent after a positive Schur
kernel is supplied. It encloses every pair cosine and Hessian coefficient on
the closed box intersected with the necessary difference constraints.
After outward rational grid rounding it charges the full spectral rounding
error. Exact positive LDL verifies a nonnegative diagonal negative-curvature
allowance eta such that the Hessian is bounded below by −diag(eta).
Numerical eigenvalues only suggest eta and never establish this fact.

At an exact feasible reference theta_ref, Taylor's theorem then supplies
a whole-domain energy lower bound using the exact reference energy, interval
gradient, nonnegative difference-constraint dual and either the full
negative-quadratic box remainder or its valid box-secant lower bound.
Every interval gradient residual is charged. The radius conversion is
outward rational and checks both neighboring squares.

A payment is accepted only if this universal lower bound is strictly greater
than Rstar_upper² sum_e w_e. Equality does not pay. Failure to find a payment
retains the entire incoming closed root.

## Required current transfer

Before removing any subtree, independently reconstruct its native/reference
prefix, exact present rods, full source angular root, every positive kernel
identity and strict bound. Match the actual published source acceptance,
radius root and current parent inventory. Retain every unattempted or
unclosed prefix and every candidate member. Independently recheck the resulting
partition, all original proofs and current bytes.

The adapter regression tests and discovery output alone perform no removal.
The source-prefix, current whole-domain acceptance and explicit transport
transfer are intentionally not replaced by a discovery boolean.
